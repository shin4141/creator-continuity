# Exposure Summary

## One-Line Description

Creator Continuity is a prompt-first handoff record for preserving creative direction across AI chats, image/video series, and agentic creator workflows.

## Pain Statement

Old chats get polluted. New chats forget.

Weak AI makes obvious mistakes.

Strong AI can drift intelligently.

## Who Should Use It

- Writers and essayists carrying voice and argument across sessions.
- Newsletter and X thread creators preserving audience promise and format.
- Image-series and short-video creators protecting visual taste, pacing, character rules, and best-known state.
- Creators using agents for overnight or repeated work who need clear boundaries before the run starts.

## What It Preserves

- Aspire
- Audience
- Voice and style
- Canon
- Best-known state
- Success patterns
- Failure patterns
- Next-AI constraints

Success and failure should be compressed into reusable continuity, not kept as private lore.

## What It Is Not

Creator Continuity is not analytics, not scoring, not a growth-hack engine, not autonomous publishing, and not a guarantee of engagement or perfect identity preservation.

It supports human creative judgment. It does not replace it. The creator keeps the Seat.

## Short X Post Draft

Old chats get polluted. New chats forget.

And strong AI can drift intelligently.

Creator Continuity is a small handoff record for carrying what should not drift: Aspire, audience, voice, canon, best-known state, success/failure patterns, and next-AI constraints.

Not automation. Not analytics. The creator keeps the Seat.
