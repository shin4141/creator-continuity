# Choose An Example

Start with the example that feels closest to the work in front of you. The examples are teaching records, not rules. Copy one, then replace the contents with your own creative direction.

## What To Replace

After copying an example, replace its contents with your own:

- Aspire: why the work exists.
- Audience: who it is for and what they need.
- Style: the voice, visual language, rhythm, format, or structure that should carry forward.
- Canon: facts, character rules, world rules, brand truths, or creative commitments that must stay true.
- Current state: the best-known state of the work right now.
- Failure patterns: what tends to go wrong when an AI continues this work.
- Next move: where the next AI should begin.
- Next-AI constraints: what the next AI should not change, invent, flatten, or optimize for.

This is prompt-first. You do not need automation, a bot, analytics, scraping, or a publishing workflow. The record is there to help the next AI session restart from your actual creative direction instead of a plausible generic version.
